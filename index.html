SIT223-SIT753 - Professional Practice in IT

Unit Content & Learning Outcomes

SIT753: Professional Practice in IT is designed to bridge the gap between technical knowledge and industry expectations. The unit content focuses on developing a deep understanding of ethical frameworks, legal responsibilities, and professional standards required in the global IT sector.

Key areas of focus include working effectively within Agile teamwork environments, managing complex stakeholder relationships, and applying critical thinking to solve professional dilemmas. Students are encouraged to reflect on their professional identity while building a portfolio that demonstrates their readiness for a career in Information Technology.
